Mind–Body Program Atlas

Popular programs that treat chronic pain, ME/CFS, long COVID, fibromyalgia, IBS, MCS, POTS, FND and related conditions as problems of brain–body regulation. Each program was researched and then fact-checked by a separate agent. Click any program to open its profile.

When programs appeared, and how many people they reach

Band thickness

Each band is one program, grouped and colored by family. Thickness is the estimated number of new people engaged each year: people who enrolled, were treated or used the program, not just people who saw content.

Estimates are order-of-magnitude. The Methods tab explains how they were derived. "Log-compressed" makes small programs visible; "People per year" shows true scale, which meditation apps dominate.

Which conditions each program claims to treat

The river plot links program families to the conditions their programs claim to treat. Width is the number of programs.

In the matrix below, dark cells mark a program's primary targets and light cells mark other claimed conditions. The ◆ / ◇ marker shows whether the program applies one mechanism to every condition (◆) or distinguishes mechanisms by condition (◇).

Program × condition matrix

Proposed mechanisms

The mind map places each program under its primary mechanism. The river plot counts every mechanism each program names.

Most programs name several mechanisms. The ones that cut across disorders cluster in two places: a threat-learning account (neuroplastic pain, limbic impairment, conditioning) and an autonomic account ("stuck in fight-or-flight", vagal tone).
