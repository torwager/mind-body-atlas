## 1. Proposed mechanism(s)

**Perceived danger.** Chronic pain and other persistent symptoms are framed as *warning signals generated by an over-protective brain* that believes the person is in danger. They are not taken as a sign of ongoing tissue damage or disease. Buglio uses "perceived danger" interchangeably with Sarno's **TMS** and Schubiner's **PPD/PDP** (psychophysiologic disorder).

**Why it persists.** Symptoms are kept going by several things:
- Fear of symptoms
- Monitoring of symptoms
- Catastrophizing
- "Watching the calendar," i.e. timelines and comparing oneself with others
- Stress
- Unresolved emotions
- Personality traits: perfectionism, people-pleasing, self-criticism

Together these keep the nervous system in **fight-or-flight**. The brain learns the pain pathway through **neuroplasticity**; the danger signal is a conditioned, learned protective response.

**How it resolves.** When the brain is convinced the body is safe, the system shifts to "rest-and-recovery" and the symptoms fade. The structural findings on MRI and X-ray (discs, stenosis, tears) are presented as usually incidental.

**Role of emotions and trauma.** Trauma, stress and "unresolved emotional wounds" are named as contributors (podcast summaries). Buglio's emphasis, however, is on present-moment *safety* and *indifference* to symptoms, not Sarno-style excavation of repressed rage.

## 2. One mechanism or many?

**A single unified mechanism.** The Symptoms page argues that "since the brain controls the entire body," perceived danger can produce almost any pain or non-pain symptom, and that "there is virtually no end" to what it can create. Pain, fatigue, GI, cardiac, skin, urological, vestibular and psychiatric symptoms are all handled the same way.

**No condition-specific mechanisms.** There are no separate mechanisms or protocols for named illnesses. Differentiation is limited to two things:

- **Screening.** The site advises seeing a doctor to rule out life-threatening or structural disease first, and concedes that some listed conditions can be organically caused.
- **Mind-body "clues."** Signs offered to suggest a symptom is mind-body:
  - no clear physical cause
  - persistence beyond normal healing (3–6 months)
  - symmetric or one-sided patterns
  - symptoms that shift or spread
  - links to stress or thoughts
  - triggering by innocuous stimuli

  The site states that answering "yes" to even one clue "means your brain is creating pain or symptoms based on perceived danger." This is a very permissive criterion, and the Join page offers "tests" for telling mind-body from structural pain.

## 3. Treatments and self-care strategies

**Core principle.** Repeatedly send the brain *safety messages*: lower fear, attention and struggle around symptoms until the brain stops generating them.

**Video course.** "The Pain Free You Roadmap" is 15 videos in three modules:
1. **Body and brain basics.** The purpose of pain, neuroplasticity, stress, and how pain becomes chronic.
2. **Knowledge and acceptance.** TMS and mind-body pain, plus documents and tests for "mind-body versus structural" pain. The aim is enough *evidence/belief* to accept the diagnosis.
3. **Roadmap to recovery.** Safety messaging, moving out of fight-or-flight, mindset, avoiding the "Freak Out Zone," shifting attention away from symptoms, and settling the nervous system.

**Self-care techniques** (named across the site, book bonuses and videos):
- **Safety reappraisal.** Reassuring self-talk, and treating sensations as "safe, just the brain."
- **Outcome independence / indifference.** Not monitoring or fighting symptoms, and not judging progress by pain levels.
- **Attention shifting** away from symptoms.
- **Graded resumption of activity.** "Six steps to resuming activity" and "Do More – Do Less" lists; feared movements are done while sending safety messages. This is effectively graded exposure.
- **Reducing the "perceived dangers" that keep the system on alert:**
  - catastrophizing
  - symptom checking
  - excessive research
  - "watching the calendar"
  - pressure, perfectionism and people-pleasing
- **Emotional work.** Reframing negative emotions (a worksheet), self-compassion, and addressing stress and unresolved emotions.
- **Mindset tools.** Positive affirmations, and handling flares and setbacks.
- **Self-assessment.** Four steps covering pain history, unresolved emotions, self-talk and personality traits, and pain experience. It is used to decide whether symptoms are mind-body.

**Group coaching dose.**
- About **12 hours per week** of live Zoom drop-in coaching (Eastern time), in windows on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday (two on Wednesday).
- A welcome call for new members.
- Sessions include advance Q&A, a "What's working?" segment for member updates, and live volunteer coaching.
- All calls are recorded for replay, and members also have the private Facebook group.
- Buglio is the only coach named.

**Duration.** The membership is open-ended month-to-month; no course length or expected time to recovery is specified.

**Free tier.** Daily short videos on YouTube and Facebook, each on a single concept or a member success story. Bios claim many people improve from the free videos alone.

## 4. Roots and influences

- **Sarno's TMS** (*Healing Back Pain*, 1991). Buglio names Sarno as the primary influence; "TMS" is used throughout.
- **The neuroplastic and PPD movement:**
  - Schubiner's *Unlearn Your Pain* (the PPD/PDP terminology)
  - Alan Gordon's **Pain Reprocessing Therapy** (somatic tracking, safety reappraisal, "the brain misinterprets safe signals as dangerous")
  - Lay "outcome independence" coaching associated with Steve Ozanich and the TMS Wiki (Buglio studied "forums, wikis")
  - The fit with these sources is inferred from his terminology; the About page cites only Sarno.
- **Pain neuroscience education.** Gate control and neuromatrix ideas (pain as a protective output of the brain), in the Moseley and Butler tradition.
- **Fear-avoidance model and graded exposure** from CBT and behavioural medicine.
- **Autonomic "fight-or-flight versus rest-and-digest"** stress framing (Selye and Cannon lineage).
- **Life-coaching and positive-psychology** style: affirmations and mindset.

## 5. Mainstream adoption and insurance

- **No formal adoption.** There is no use in health systems, guidelines or payers, and no CMS, NICE or guideline mentions were found.
- **Within the TMS/neuroplastic community (ATNS, formerly the PPD Association)**, Buglio is a recognized lay voice. He has an accepted session at the 2026 ATNS Conference, "Simplifying Mind-Body Symptom Recovery: A Practical Framework for Teaching the Brain Safety," and appears on many health podcasts.
- **No insurance coverage.** The membership is a direct-pay subscription, and the coaching is not delivered by a licensed clinician.

## 6. Evidence base

**No trials of the program.**
- No peer-reviewed trials, observational outcome studies or published case series exist for Pain Free You.
- PubMed has nothing indexed under "Buglio" with pain or mind-body terms; a "Pain Free You" phrase search returns only unrelated papers (re-checked 2026-10-12).
- ClinicalTrials.gov has no registrations.
- The site claims "over 30 years of research" but cites no studies.
- Efficacy claims rest on testimonials and member success-story videos.

**Related generic techniques that have trials.** These are *not* trials of the program:
- **Pain Reprocessing Therapy** (Ashar et al., *JAMA Psychiatry* 2022; RCT, N=151, chronic back pain). About 66% of the PRT group were pain-free or nearly pain-free at post-treatment, against about 20% of placebo-injection controls. That trial used licensed therapists over 8 sessions plus a physician visit.
- **Emotional Awareness and Expression Therapy** for fibromyalgia (Lumley et al., *Pain* 2017; RCT, N=230).
- **Pain neuroscience education** (systematic reviews show modest benefits).

**Gaps and critiques.**
- No outcome tracking, no defined dose, and no adverse-event reporting.
- Self-selected testimonials are the only evidence.
- The very broad list of claimed conditions goes well beyond the populations studied in PRT and EAET trials. Those trials enrolled primary chronic pain, not RA, autoimmune disease, hEDS or MCAS.

  - A private members' Facebook group also exists.
- **2026: ATNS conference speaker.** A sign of growing standing within the neuroplastic-symptom field.
- **Reddit and forums.** Commonly recommended alongside Sarno, Schubiner, Gordon, Curable and the TMS Wiki on r/TMSnoMore and r/ChronicPain. This is a qualitative impression; it was not quantified this session.

**Trajectory.** Steady growth since 2019, with a step up around the 2025 book launch. Reach is mid-sized compared with Curable or Sarno, but substantial for a single lay coach.

## 8. Controversies / criticisms

- **Lay coach with no clinical credentials.** He offers diagnosis-by-checklist ("mind-body vs. structural") and states that pain can be banished "regardless of your X-Rays or MRI results." Critics of the TMS/neuroplastic-pain movement warn that this risks missed or delayed diagnosis.
- **An extremely broad list of conditions.** It includes RA, arthritis, autoimmune disease, hEDS, MCAS, SIBO, EBV reactivation, asthma and allergies, "leaky gut," long COVID and ME/CFS/PEM.
  - These are framed as possibly perceived-danger symptoms. Attributing post-exertional malaise to perceived danger is contested by ME/CFS patient groups.
  - The site does caveat that some cases are organic and advises a medical check first.
- **No published outcomes.** Results rest on testimonials; there is no disclosed success rate, guarantee or refund policy.
- **Subscription model.** An open-ended US$100/month subscription for group coaching, and the cost accumulates over long recoveries.
- **No public scandals found** (limited by the search budget).
