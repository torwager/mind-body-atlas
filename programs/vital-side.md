## 1. Proposed mechanism(s)
- **Conditioned chronic stress response.** Chronic symptoms are said to come from a nervous system stuck in fight-flight-freeze, or "survival mode." The sympathetic system stays "ON," which blocks the parasympathetic "rest-and-digest / growth-and-repair" state. From 2020 to 2022 the site's tagline was a shift from "fight-or-flight to growth-and-repair."
- **"Limbic system impairment" (LSI).** The site borrows this term, popularised by DNRS (Annie Hopper). The idea is that after an accumulated toxic, infectious or emotional load, the brain's threat circuitry is conditioned to over-respond to benign stimuli such as foods, chemicals, exertion and sensations. This is said to perpetuate symptoms after the original trigger has gone.
- **Neuroplasticity and "epigenetics."** Repeated mental exercises are claimed to rewire these circuits. The site also claims that retraining thought patterns can change gene expression, with no citations, and talks loosely about "brain waves" and "DOSE" neurochemicals (dopamine, oxytocin, serotonin, endorphins).
- **Body heals itself once the brain is retrained.** Once the brain moves out of survival mode, the body is said to "do the rest." The founder's own story frames real infections and toxins as the original triggers, with brain conditioning keeping the illness going.
- Polyvagal theory, the amygdala and the cell danger response are not explicitly invoked on the main pages.

## 2. One mechanism or many?
**Single unified mechanism.** A list of 50+ diagnoses and symptoms (Lyme, mould, MCAS, POTS, ME/CFS, GI, hormonal, anxiety and so on) is all attributed to the same conditioned survival-state and limbic/ANS dysregulation. The program does not offer condition-specific models. Personalisation is framed as "bio-individual," meaning which triggers and symptoms each person targets, not as a different pathophysiology. Organic diagnoses such as Lyme and RA are not excluded; the site presents them as conditions that retraining has been applied to.

## 3. Treatments and self-care strategies
**Rewire (core program, 6-month practice frame inside a 12-month membership):**
- **Video course.** More than 10 hours, suggested to be finished in 10–14 days, plus a 64-page personalised guidebook. Modules: (1) Gain Awareness; (2–3) Develop Acceptance; (4–5) Learn How to Take Action.
- **"The 7 Steps."** The proprietary core retraining sequence, with extended and audio versions. Like other limbic-retraining protocols, it interrupts the symptom or trigger response and redirects attention to positive emotional states and visualisation. A longer technique of about 20 minutes is also included.
- **"State changers."** Brief interrupts used the moment a symptom appears.
- **"Incidental training."** Tools used through the day.
- **"Incremental Progress."** Graded re-exposure to avoided foods, chemicals and activities.
- **Dose.** 20–60 minutes of daily practice for 6 months.
- **Adjunct tools:**
  - EFT/tapping
  - NLP techniques
  - visualisation
  - breathing
  - movement
  - laughter yoga
  - limbic morning and nighttime meditations (about 34 minutes each) and a 6-minute "reminder" meditation
- **Support.** Private community, staff messaging, roughly monthly coach-led group calls, paid Deep Dives (90 minutes) and paid 1:1 sessions (a 75-minute "Mastermind," hourly sessions or a 3-session package).

**Other products:**
- **Reset.** A 7-day intro course: 7 videos of 7 minutes or less.
- **Reboot.** A 3-part course for life transitions and stress.
- **Elevate ("Brain Retraining 2.0," with Bianca Spears).** Six phases over 6–12 weeks, 38 tools, a 68-page guidebook, and an emphasis on positive psychology, NLP and laughter wellness, for experienced retrainers.
- **Regulation Station.** Somatic "state changer" tools and live events.
- **Children's coaching.** For ages 6 and up. Parents are told to model regulation.

## 4. Roots and influences
- **Limbic retraining.** DNRS (Hopper, about 2008) and the Gupta Program (about 2007). The site uses the DNRS term "limbic system impairment" and markets Elevate to DNRS, Gupta, Lightning Process and Rocksteady graduates.
- **The founder's training.** NLP (Bandler and Grinder), EFT (Craig) and TFT (Callahan).
- **Other influences:**
  - laughter yoga (Kataria)
  - positive psychology
  - guided visualisation and meditation
  - popular neuroplasticity and epigenetics writing in the Dispenza/Lipton style (her terminology resembles it, but she does not name them)
- **Implicit models.** Stress physiology (sympathetic/parasympathetic balance) and classical conditioning.
- **Milieu.** Functional and integrative medicine: the Lyme, mould and MCAS illness-identity communities.

## 5. Mainstream adoption and insurance
I found no hospital, health-system, guideline (NICE, CDC, etc.) or payer adoption. The program is sold direct-to-consumer. The founder's PA-C credential gives it clinical framing, but the program is explicitly "not medical advice" and coaching is non-clinical. It is not insurance reimbursable, and the site does not mention HSA/FSA.

## 6. Evidence base
- **Branded program.** PubMed searches for "vital-side" or "vital side" with brain retraining returned 0 records (re-run 2026-10-13). A broader author search on Mitchell L with brain retraining returned 9 records, none relevant. ClinicalTrials.gov has no registered trial (API query, 0 studies, 2026-10-13). No case series, cohort or outcome audit has been published.
- **Company claims.** "Over 10,000 people" helped and "92% of members see a massive transformation." No method, denominator or follow-up is given; these are presumably member self-reports. Evidence presented is testimonials, practitioner endorsements, the founder's own recovery and podcast interviews.
- **Related generic techniques.** Trials of these do not test Vital-Side itself. Among peer limbic-retraining programs, DNRS has only uncontrolled or observational data, Gupta has a small pilot RCT, and the Lightning Process has one contested RCT in adolescents with CFS (SMILE; Crawley et al., 2018). Graded exposure and CBT-type approaches for MCS/IEI and functional disorders have a modest RCT base. EFT and NLP have contested evidence. No data exist on the specific 7-Steps protocol.
- **Gap.** There is no independent or investigator-initiated evaluation. The no-refund, auto-renewing model, plus outcome claims without data, means efficacy is unknown.

## 7. Popularity and trajectory
- **2017–2018.** Concept in 2017. Squarespace site captured from Aug 2018 (Wayback).
- **2020–2022.** Moved to Shopify. Marketed as an "individualized, self-directed, non-pharmaceutical brain retraining program." Price was USD 279 in Sept 2020. Growth probably accelerated with long COVID: the team now includes a coach who recovered from long COVID and ME/CFS.
- **About 2021 onward.** The *Rewire* podcast, later called *The Vital Side Podcast*, reached 180 episodes by Oct 2025.
- **2023–2026.** Product line expanded (Elevate, Reset, Reboot, Regulation Station, children's coaching). Price rose to USD 397/year. More than 10,000 participants claimed cumulatively.
- **Relative size.** It sits in the mid tier of the brain-retraining market: smaller than DNRS or Gupta and larger than one-coach outfits. Its roughly 62K Instagram followers (Oct 2026) are the best hard indicator of reach. Its large Instagram following contrasts with a small Facebook page (about 1.6K). The program is mentioned alongside DNRS, Gupta and Primal Trust in MCAS, mould and long-COVID patient forums, but Reddit could not be quantified. Reddit's JSON endpoints returned HTTP 403 for every subreddit queried, and web search was unavailable.

## 8. Controversies / criticisms
- Claims to address organic diseases (Lyme, RA, MCAS, mould/CIRS, "hormonal imbalance") and 50+ conditions with a single brain-retraining model, without outcome data.
- Unreferenced claims that thought patterns change gene expression, and that the power to heal from within is "a scientific fact."
- Leans on the PA-C credential while disclaiming medical advice. Coaching staff have non-clinical backgrounds (life coaching, public health, holistic health/homeopathy).
- No-refund, auto-renewing subscription. Discount-code marketing.
- Shares the general criticism of limbic-retraining programs from ME/CFS advocacy groups: they imply illness is perpetuated by the brain and attribute non-response to insufficient practice. I found no specific published critique of Vital-Side.
