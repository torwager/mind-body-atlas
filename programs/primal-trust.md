## 1. Proposed mechanism(s)
- **Core claim.** Chronic symptoms come from a *dysregulated nervous system* stuck in a protective stress or survival state. "Triggers" of four kinds keep it in that state: chemical, pathogenic, physical and emotional.
- **Limbic/brain layer ("top-down").** The limbic system, especially the amygdala, has been conditioned to over-detect threat. The resulting loops of symptom, fear and hypervigilance perpetuate illness. Retraining is said to rewire these loops through neuroplasticity ("neurons that fire together wire together").
- **Autonomic/polyvagal layer ("bottom-up").** The program uses Porges' polyvagal theory as a map of states (ventral vagal safety, sympathetic fight/flight, dorsal shutdown/freeze). Chronic illness is framed as being locked in sympathetic or dorsal states. Vagal toning and somatic work are meant to restore "safety".
- **Cellular layer.** It invokes Naviaux's *cell danger response*: cells stay in a defensive metabolic mode while the organism perceives threat. This links nervous-system state to fatigue, sensitivities and immune symptoms. The site asserts the link but doesn't elaborate or cite it.
- **Trauma/attachment layer.** Early developmental trauma and attachment wounds are held to underlie the dysregulated baseline in many members. Level 2 targets "core wounds" through parts work and memory reconsolidation.
- **Not purely psychological.** The program acknowledges real pathogens, toxins and mould as triggers, while giving nervous-system state the decisive causal role in staying ill.

## 2. One mechanism or many?
**Mostly one unified mechanism, with emphasis varying by condition.** ME/CFS, Lyme, long COVID, mould illness, MCS and EHS, chronic pain, POTS, autoimmunity, anxiety and PTSD all fall under the same nervous-system-dysregulation, limbic-conditioning and cell-danger-response model. The differences are in which triggers dominate, such as chemical/environmental for MCS and mould, pathogenic for Lyme and COVID, and emotional or developmental for trauma presentations, and in pacing for low-function members. FAQ outcome figures are split by condition (Lyme vs. long COVID/CFS/mould), but the intervention is the same. There are no condition-specific pathophysiological subtypes, for example no POTS subtypes.

## 3. Treatments and self-care strategies
**Structure (all included in membership):**
- **Daily Regulate (first run Feb 2026).** 40 days of 5–7 minute videos with daily emails. Entry-level.
- **Level 1 – Regulate.** 7 modules of 1–3 hours each with workbooks, released weekly. Recommended pace is 2–3 months, or 4–6 months with cognitive impairment. Content covers:
  - *ABC Dimensional Shift*. King's limbic-retraining routine (developed 2017) for interrupting a symptom or fear response and rehearsing a calm or healthy state. It is structurally similar to DNRS/Gupta "stop–reframe–elevate" routines.
  - Polyvagal state mapping and self-awareness.
  - Vagus nerve toning: breath, voice/humming, eye exercises.
  - Somatic movement and presencing: Feldenkrais/Hanna-style pandiculation and orienting.
  - Breathwork.
  - Positive-state "elevation" practices. HeartMath-style coherence is mentioned by members.
  - Paced lifestyle changes.
- **Level 2 – Integrate (mentorship).** For emotional triggers and trauma patterns: parts work, inquiry, attachment repair, developing an "Adult Main Personality (AMP)", memory-reconsolidation "brain-rewiring attunements". Plan for 2+ months. The company values it at about USD 1,500 as a standalone product.
- **Level 3 – Liberate.** Ongoing weekly live mentoring plus an advanced library.
- **Live classes, nearly daily.** Body-Brain Neural Reset, Elevate & Regulate, Somatic Discovery/Inquiry, journaling, sound healing ("Sound Mezze"), Q&As and attunements with Dr. Cat, guest teachers, affinity groups.
- **Social support.** Guide-led study pods, community forum (weekdays), free calls with ambassadors.
- **Dose.** 30–60+ minutes per day of practice spread across the day, increasing by module. King reports doing 1–1.5 hours per day herself. Expected healing timeline is 6–12 months for long-term illness, with no guarantee.
- **Disclaimers.** Not a substitute for medical care. Staff are not medical professionals and don't advise on supplements or protocols.

## 4. Roots and influences
- **Limbic retraining.** Hopper's DNRS and Gupta's Amygdala Retraining (the same structure of interrupting the response and rehearsing a positive state).
- **Polyvagal theory** (Porges) and **Somatic Experiencing**-style regulation and titration.
- **Somatics.** Feldenkrais and Hanna Somatics, both named in King's training.
- **Sarno TMS / mind-body pain.** Sarno is named in King's training.
- **Memory reconsolidation** (Ecker/Coherence Therapy lineage), **PSYCH-K** (energy-psychology/belief-change, close to NLP), **parts work** (IFS-like), attachment theory.
- **Cell danger response** (Naviaux) from functional/integrative medicine, plus the Lyme/mould (CIRS) patient ecosystem.
- **Breathwork, HeartMath-style coherence, sound healing** (yoga/pranayama-adjacent wellness practices).

## 6. Evidence base
- **Branded-program trials:** none. PubMed's phrase index doesn't contain "primal trust". The 26 word-match results are all unrelated (e.g., "primal world beliefs"), and ClinicalTrials.gov returned 0 studies (Oct 2026).
- **Internal outcome claims (FAQ/membership pages):**
  - "87% improvement" in self-identified low-function members at 3 months
  - "60% average improvement" at 6 months of tool use
  - "51%" for Lyme at 3 months
  - about 40% for long COVID, CFS and mould at 3 months

  No methodology, denominators, attrition, outcome measure or comparator are reported. These are self-selected, self-reported figures and can't be interpreted as efficacy.
- **The site's "Sources/Read the studies" section** had no studies listed when fetched.
- **Related generic evidence (not for this program):** Limbic-retraining programs have only weak evidence (e.g., Gupta Program pilot data). Polyvagal theory's physiological premises are contested. Somatic/mind-body approaches such as PRT and EAET have RCT support in chronic pain, but not in ME/CFS, MCS, EHS or Lyme populations as delivered here.

## 8. Controversies / criticisms
- **Shared with limbic-retraining programs in general.** ME/CFS patient advocates object to framing post-exertional illness as nervous-system conditioning, citing:
  - a risk of pushing activity beyond the person's limits
  - blame when people don't recover ("not doing it right")
  - testimonial-driven marketing
- **Unsupported claims.** Outcome percentages are published without methodology. A "studies" section is empty. Polyvagal theory and the cell danger response are invoked as settled science despite scientific dispute (polyvagal) or speculative extension (CDR applied to MCS/EHS).
- **Scope.** Very broad condition list (autoimmune disease, infections, EHS) with one mechanism.
- **Business model.** A recurring subscription with a 6–12-month expected horizon creates an incentive to keep members long-term.
- **Credentials.** Staff and mentors are explicitly not medical professionals, and trauma processing is delivered by non-clinician mentors.
