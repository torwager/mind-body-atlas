## 1. Proposed mechanism(s)
- **Core claim.** These illnesses are neurological. Persistent autonomic nervous system (ANS) dysregulation (sympathetic overdrive, "survival mode") plus central sensitisation are the root drivers.
- **Onset.** A combination of physical and psychological stressors starts the illness. Listed triggers: infection, vaccination, accident, surgery, childbirth, over-exercise, emotionally stressful events. Neuffer's own case combined vaccination with a stressful year.
- **Perpetuation.** The ANS stays "stuck" after the trigger resolves. It misreads normal internal and external signals as threats, a conditioned (classical-conditioning) threat response. The site says the limbic system and hypothalamus control the ANS, drawing on Teitelbaum's hypothalamic framing.
- **Secondary dysfunctions.** These sit downstream of ANS dysregulation and lowered immunity: mitochondrial and metabolic dysfunction, endocrine (HPA) changes, infection load, gut dysfunction, hypoglycaemia, neurotransmitter imbalance, methylation, cardiac issues, mineral deficits.
  - They then act as ongoing *physical triggers* that close a vicious circle.
  - The site uses this hybrid physical-plus-brain loop to argue that its model is not "psychosomatic."
- **Explaining other programs' results.** Recoveries on CBT or limbic-retraining programs are read as ANS normalisation with secondary resolution. Non-responders are attributed to unaddressed physical triggers.
- **Cited science.** The site cites studies showing that ANS abnormalities are *present* in these groups, e.g. HRV and orthostatic findings (Allen 2012; Stewart 1998; De Becker 1998; Naschitz 2004; Martínez-Martínez 2014). None shows that dysautonomia is *causal*, or that retraining reverses it.

## 2. One mechanism or many?
Essentially **one unified mechanism, with condition-specific emphasis**. The site says outright that ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, POTS, MCS, EHS, IBS, PVFS and "adrenal fatigue" are the same illness with different labels.
- **Fibromyalgia.** Adds pain desensitisation, since pain is treated as inflammation plus central sensitisation and is itself a trigger.
- **MCS / EHS.** Treated as acquired, conditioned sensory triggers. They get targeted REWIRE work and cognitive "re-contextualisation."
- **POTS.** Treated as a visible expression of the same ANS dysfunction. No subtyping (hypovolaemic, neuropathic, hyperadrenergic).
- **ME/CFS.** The default case. The exercise "conundrum" (post-exertional worsening versus benefit) is handled with pacing guidance.
- **Exclusions.** The Science page says long COVID with organ damage (lung, heart, kidney) should seek other options. Structural brain injury and children ("designed for adults only") come only from replies in the page's comments, not formal criteria; the Program Details page lists no exclusions.

## 3. Treatments and self-care strategies
The structure is about 6 weeks of daily lessons of 25–35 minutes each. Participants are asked to commit to 6 months and are told to expect improvement over 3–6 months. The stated sequence is: (1) reduce triggering, (2) build a foundation for healing and neuroplasticity, (3) retrain.

- **Understanding (weeks 1–2).** Education on the ANS and the illness model. Self-assessment of onset history, symptoms and function. Recovery targets.
- **Physical strategies.**
  - Addressing "4 main physical triggers," not named publicly.
  - Dietary change, gut support, nutrition, sleep hygiene.
  - Breathing retraining to reduce sympathetic arousal.
  - Pacing and rest to avoid boom–bust cycles.
  - Graded, individualised activity.
  - Posture and movement adjustments.
  - "Somato-neurological" body-to-brain techniques.
  - "4 pillars of energy restoration."
- **Brain training.**
  - Neuroplasticity education.
  - Mindfulness meditation course (guided 20/40/60-minute tracks).
  - Relaxation-response training.
  - Pain desensitisation built on "3 elements."
  - Cognitive re-framing of pain and chemical sensitivity.
  - **The REWIRE technique**, a limbic-retraining-style routine. You interrupt the habitual response to a trigger, change how you relate to it, then redirect attention. It is applied to physical-expression triggers, conscious psychological triggers and subconscious psychological triggers.
  - People with a trauma history are referred to a therapist (Somatic Experiencing and EFT are mentioned as options).
  - "Brain games" are discouraged.
- **Audio.** Meditation, two pain-desensitisation sessions and two sleep-relaxation tracks. Many tracks use "brainwave entrainment" (binaural, isochronic and monaural beats).
- **Optional medical adjuncts**, pursued with an integrative MD or naturopath: cellular-metabolism support, hormonal and neurotransmitter support, detox support.
- **Coaching and behavioural tools.** Daily action steps, symptom and activity tracking, flexible goals, an accountability partner, reframing setbacks, values and life balance.

## 4. Roots and influences
- **Founder's own recovery.** Gut and elimination diet, supplements (magnesium, B vitamins, CoQ10), meditation, and "mental techniques" to retrain the ANS (Health Rising 2015). The claim that these were NLP-derived (as in the Lightning Process) was not verified at fact-check and should be treated as an inference.
- **Limbic-retraining lineage.** Close structural parallels to DNRS (Hopper) and the Gupta Program: pattern interrupt, then reframe, then redirect attention.
- **Mindfulness and meditation research.** Relaxation Response / Benson; MBSR-style meditation.
- **Pain science.** Central sensitisation and neuroplasticity framing. Thieme's operant and psychophysiological fibromyalgia work is cited via her endorsement.
- **Behaviourism / classical conditioning.** This is the explanation for persistence.
- **Selye-style stress physiology.** Stress and survival-mode framing.
- **Functional / integrative medicine.** Teitelbaum's hypothalamic model, plus mitochondria, methylation, adrenal and detox adjuncts.
- **Biofeedback-era entrainment.** Brainwave-entrainment audio.

## 5. Mainstream adoption and insurance
- No adoption by any health system, hospital, or guideline body. NICE NG206 (ME/CFS, 2021) specifically cautions against therapies based on deconditioning or presented as a cure; such programs fall outside it.
- No CPT/billing pathway, no clinician delivery and no insurance coverage. Users pay directly.
- Its visibility in patient communities comes through Health Rising (Cort Johnson's site, which has hosted Neuffer's articles and recovery story) and long-COVID podcasts.

## 6. Evidence base
- **Trials of the branded program: none.**
  - PubMed: no hits for "ANS Rewire" or Neuffer plus CFS.
  - ClinicalTrials.gov: zero studies.
  - No published audits, cohort data or success rates.
- **Founder's stance.** He has reportedly said he lacked resources for a publishable study (date/source not re-verified). The FAQ argues that blinded RCTs are unsuitable for an individualised program and declines to give a success rate.
- **Related generic evidence (not the branded program).** Mindfulness, relaxation, pacing and pain-education components each have some RCT support in fibromyalgia and chronic pain. Limbic-retraining analogues (DNRS, Gupta) have only small or pilot studies. None of this tests the ANS-causal model or the REWIRE technique.
- **Gap.** The efficacy case rests entirely on testimonials (e.g. "10% to 100% functional in about 6 months") and practitioner endorsements, with no independent evaluation.

## 7. Popularity and trajectory
- **Before launch (c. 2013–2015).** *CFS Unravelled* and the cfsunravelled.com blog built an audience. Health Rising featured the recovery story in Aug 2015.
- **Launch (2016).** First Wayback snapshot of ansrewire.com in March 2016.
- **Growth (2017–2019).** Edition of *CFS Unravelled* with Thieme's foreword, the *Discover Hope* book, and a regular podcast (on player.fm in several languages).
- **Long-COVID boost (2020–2023).** Neuffer pivoted messaging toward long COVID and PVFS, appearing on the Long Covid Podcast and on YouTube ("New treatments for ME/CFS & Long Covid targeting ANS dysfunction").
- **2024–2026.** Steady niche presence.
- **Current claims.** "Thousands" of participants across 60+ countries and 5,000+ answered member questions.
- **Relative size.** Visibility on Reddit and in Google Trends appears far lower than for DNRS, Gupta, Primal Trust or Curable (not verified quantitatively this session).
- **Rough reach estimate.** Paid participants are probably in the low thousands to about 10k. Including book and podcast audiences, reach is perhaps 10^4–10^5.

## 8. Controversies / criticisms
- **Patient-advocate objections.** Like other "brain retraining" programs for ME/CFS, it draws criticism that it implies a psychological cause and that recovery testimonials pressure patients. Neuffer counters with the hybrid physical-loop model.
- **Refusal to collect outcome data.** The case against RCTs is a standard criticism point.
- **Selective citation.** Correlational ANS studies are used as support for a causal claim.
- **Lumping.** Heterogeneous conditions, including "adrenal fatigue," which endocrinology does not recognise, plus "chronic Lyme" and mould illness, are combined under one cause.
- **Non-evidence-based adjuncts.** Brainwave entrainment, detox and methylation support.
- **Founder credentials.** He has no clinical qualifications, which matters given that coaching is sold.
- **Unverified.** No regulatory actions or formal complaints were found in this session.
